title: "Esports Engagement at Mega-Events"
summary: >-
  Esports engagement across mega-events in Australia are becoming widely popular to attend. Societal configuration of the esports ecosystem has come with a cultural stamp, due to the persistence of masculine sporting cultures infiltrating esports communities. Sporting culture cannot be ignored when engaging with esports events, as the field itself produces similar pipelines present in sporting culture. Alongside esports, an important parallel to consider when understanding the formation of videogame communities and their longstanding marginalisation of women and gender diverse individuals in this space. Hence, the importance that esports engagement at mega-events has particularly with a focus on gender, can help us understand why videogame and esports engagement at mega-events is struggling to support women and gender diverse individuals in the space, but culturally how we can dismantle the current culture and the persistent issues when playing games, such as identity-based exclusion and harassment whether participating at physical events casually or online and LAN tournaments (Taylor, 2012; Witkowski & Harkin, 2025; Zhu, 2025). In hopes to illuminate the possibility of how esports events can encourage a new generation of players, research focusing on marginalised perspectives will greatly benefit the industry's understanding of how these spaces can come with better practices to create equal opportunity for society and create cultural change in a historically hetero-masculine and cis-white male space.
