title: "Clipping Bodies: Embodied poetry in n00bs guide"
summary: >-
  This talk offers a new way of looking at game narratives by combining designed experiences with metaphors in the written word. Using n00bs guide as a case study, Erica examines how its narrative pairs experience design with narrative writing. Focusing on moments embodied by the player, the talk explores the intertwining of metaphors that are experienced and written about, contributing to the emerging discourse around games poetry.
