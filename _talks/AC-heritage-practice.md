title: "Roleplaying Games as Heritage Practice from an Australian Perspective"
summary: >-
  Roleplaying games often serve as vehicles for self-exploration grounded in cultural, personal and historical touchstones. They also create artefacts of play, including character sheets, props, research, notes and other paraphernalia. This session explores historical and alternate-history settings and considers how techniques such as historical photography and found objects can inspire magic systems, worldbuilding and play.
