title: "Ludolio: A New Platform for Distributing Games in Education"
summary: >-
  Games are widely recognised as significant cultural objects, but bringing them into university teaching remains difficult. Ludolio was built to remove the access problem by licensing commercial games to universities so staff can assign them as readings, playable on students’ own devices. This panel reflects on its first year in classrooms across media and communication, education and game-design units, discussing what changed once access stopped being the barrier, what students made of short and formally unusual games outside the commercial mainstream, and what remains difficult.
