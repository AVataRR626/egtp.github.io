title: "“Flash Is Dead.” What Is Your Game’s Afterlife?"
summary: >-
  What happens when a game really dies—when there is no storefront, no distribution and perhaps not even a licence? Flash is one story of games being kept alive long after official support. Through real-world examples of games still thriving beyond their expiry date, this talk explores why longevity matters and what people in every project role can do to support a game’s afterlife after it leaves their hands.
