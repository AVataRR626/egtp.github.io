  Every year, thousands more games launch on Steam, making the cost of betting on the wrong idea increasingly high. At Chaos Theory Games, James and the team built a rapid validation pipeline and released more than ten browser-playable prototypes for PC and console concepts during 2026, each in roughly two weeks. This practical talk breaks down the pipeline from concept generation and playtesting to release and data-driven go/no-go decisions, including which Itch.io metrics matter, how WebGL can increase plays and how to distinguish a weak concept from weak marketing.
