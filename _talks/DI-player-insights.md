title: "Video Game Player Insights From 5 Countries: Lessons for Game Dev"
summary: >-
  This talk unpacks a detailed survey of more than 3,600 video game players across five countries, commissioned by Screen Australia for its Screen Currency 2025 project. It explores genre preferences, play locations, information sources and seven distinct market segments, with a closer look at the USA, Canada, India and the UK. The findings offer practical guidance for understanding players and applying audience insight throughout development, grant applications, communication, demo releases and launch.
