title: "The 7/10 Problem: Towards A Standardised Scoring System In Game Reviews"
summary: >-
  This light and humorous presentation pokes fun at the persistent myth in gaming culture that review scores are weighted too frequently to be a 7/10. Through an investigation of why this happens, Ryan proposes a “solution” in the form of a standardised five-star, no-half-star rating system that will elevate game reviews to the status they should have always had.
